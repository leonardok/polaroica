The first release of **Polaroica**: browse, cull, edit and export your photo libraries (local folders and SMB network shares) inside VS Code.

## Install

Download `polaroica-0.10.5.vsix` below, then in VS Code: **Extensions** view → `…` menu → **Install from VSIX…** (or run `code --install-extension polaroica-0.10.5.vsix`). Requires VS Code 1.85 or later.

## What's in it

- **Libraries and albums**: add local folders or network shares; albums show their photo count, and hovering one shows picks, rejects, photos still to cull, stars, edits and RAW/JPEG counts.
- **Grid and photo tabs**: fast thumbnails (from the camera's embedded previews where possible), EXIF details, a filmstrip, keyboard navigation.
- **Culling**: Pick (`P`), Reject (`X`), 0–5 stars, filters for picks / unflagged / rejects and by stars.
- **Auto-Rank**: rates an album by focus on the subject, blown highlights, eyes open, looking at the camera and smiles; groups bursts and similar shots, picks the best of each and keeps near-ties. Everything runs on your computer; nothing is uploaded.
- **Adjust**: exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, temperature (Kelvin), tint, vibrance and saturation, with before/after, undo and copy/paste settings. Non-destructive: edits live in `.sidecar/<photo>.xmp`, the photo file is never changed.
- **Export to JPEG**: export the selection, picks, by stars or everything but rejects, at full size or for sharing. Edits are baked in. **RAW files are developed at full sensor resolution** (LibRaw) with the camera's own colours and tones. Exports carry EXIF, an embedded thumbnail and a 1600 px preview so they browse instantly.
- **Delete rejects**: send an album's rejected photos to the Trash (asks again before deleting permanently on shares without a Trash).
- **Move** photos and folders between albums and libraries; edits move with them.

## Known limitations

- RAW export doesn't apply the camera's lens corrections, so it frames slightly wider than the camera JPEG.
- Grid thumbnails don't show edits yet.
- File-change watching may not fire on every SMB server; use **Refresh**.
